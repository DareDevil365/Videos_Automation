# Video Automation

Maintain [PROJECT_GUIDE.md](PROJECT_GUIDE.md) when the architecture or workflows change.

For video work, start with the [assistant production handoff](docs/ASSISTANT_PRODUCTION.md). It points to the production history, the next idea, the ready previews, and the genre playbooks. For comic lore, use the [comic-lore playbook](docs/categories/comic-lore/HOW_TO_CREATE.md), [visual examples](docs/categories/comic-lore/VISUAL_STYLE.md), and [research and reach workflow](docs/categories/comic-lore/RESEARCH_AND_REACH.md).

Use these examples to judge the result. Each new story should have its own composition, setting, and rhythm. Keep local production records, assets, and credentials in their existing local folders.
