Use bold, accurate curiosity hooks. “Does the same actor make Doom an Iron Man variant?” is a question the video answers. Withholding a material eligibility limit or presenting a rumor, old result or reported estimate as confirmed would change what the viewer thinks the video promises. Keep those qualifications in the actual script and packaging. [YouTube metadata guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en) is also relevant.

Source collection for lore: publisher reading order, issue/collection listings and primary character histories first. Community sites can suggest issues to check; they do not establish canon or asset rights. Separate original novels, expanded canon, comic runs and screen adaptations. The 2015 Secret Wars sequence is not a film prediction, and the proposed Odyssey/Dune ancestry link remains unverified.
