# Create: myth versus fact

Preset `myth_fact`, education channel, 25–45 seconds, 65–115 spoken words and 6–12 units. Every scene uses original supported typography with the accepted education voice.

1. Research one specific misconception/question and a bounded correction using actual primary-source evidence. A claim about what people commonly believe also needs a basis; do not invent consensus.
2. Label the incorrect proposition as a misconception or question at the opening. Deliver the supported correction and explain it with a concrete example and any relevant limit. Never relabel an unsupported fact as advice/opinion to bypass review.
3. Use a new [playbook batch plan](../README.md#shared-playbook-batch-recipe), `playbook: "myth_fact"`, `channel: "education"`, actual source inputs and an initial `test_only: true`. Graphics and enhanced editorial defaults are selected automatically; no lower panel is allowed, music is optional.
4. The director supplies a graphic on every beat and at least one comparison with `headings: ["Myth", "Fact"]` and two exact script phrases. Adding a heading alone does not supply the claim/correction or its evidence.
5. Run the plan, inspect the actual comparison, spoken correction, evidence/reviews and whole video. There is no demonstrated conversion or comment-rate advantage for this format.

Read [editorial/speech behavior](../../EDITORIAL_AND_SPEECH.md) and [exported writer rules](../../../Resources/Prompts/myth_fact.writer.json). A model's support verdict remains fallible; source accuracy and creative quality still need review.
