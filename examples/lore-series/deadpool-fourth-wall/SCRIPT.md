# Deadpool’s real target

**u1 — Host**

Deadpool killed the Avengers. But they weren't his real target.

**u2 — Narrator**

In Deadpool Kills the Marvel Universe, from 2012, Wade's mission starts with an attempt to control him.

**u3 — Narrator**

Psycho-Man poses as a doctor and tries to brainwash him. Instead, a new voice convinces Wade his world is fiction, and everyone's suffering is entertainment.

**u4 — Narrator**

Wade decides killing the characters will free them. Stolen shrinking technology lets him smuggle explosives into the Avengers' headquarters.

**u5 — Narrator**

Hulk tears him apart. Wade regenerates, waits for Hulk to become Bruce Banner, then kills him.

**u6 — Narrator**

But killing everyone only destroys the cast. It doesn't touch whoever controls their lives, so Wade needs a way outside the story.

**u7 — Narrator**

At the Nexus of All Realities, a gateway between worlds, Wade reaches Man-Thing. The creature opens a portal, and Wade steps through.

**u8 — Narrator**

On the other side, he finds people planning the very comic we're reading. His next targets are its writers and artists.

**u9 — Host**

Then he turns toward the reader. After the heroes and their creators, we're next. For more stories like this, subscribe.
