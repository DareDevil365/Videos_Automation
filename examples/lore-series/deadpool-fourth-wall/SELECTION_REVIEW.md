# Choosing the Deadpool story

Checked 7 November 2026. The goal is a concrete opening, escalation and a late consequence. Audience traction is an editorial hypothesis; this channel has no connected performance measurements.

| Candidate | Opening and payoff | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deadpool Kills the Marvel Universe (2012) | Destroying the heroes still cannot reach the people controlling their suffering; Wade reaches the comic's creators, then addresses the reader. | Selected: recognizable Avengers/Hulk, a causal motive, an action reversal and a distinctly Deadpool final turn. |
| Thanos makes Wade immortal out of jealousy | An apparent gift keeps Wade separated from Death. | Strong future angle; verify the precise 2002 curse and later removal before writing, rather than claiming permanent current immortality. |
| Deadpool steals Dracula's intended bride | A paid delivery becomes a romance that ruins Dracula's plan. | Strong contrasting adventure/romance candidate; read the exact escape and wedding sequence first. |
| Deadpool helps Danielle in issue #20 | The violent mercenary chooses to stay and help someone in crisis. | A different emotional register; requires restrained treatment and the full resolution. Not selected for this action/horror request. |
| Deadpool vs. Carnage | Unpredictability challenges another chaotic killer. | Familiar opponents but the exact winning mechanism still needs reading; a fight alone is not a story. |
| Deadpool travels into an old Spider-Man issue | The modern mercenary disrupts a classic comic's world. | Visual/comedy possibilities; needs the actual issue and original jokes rather than copied creator commentary. |

Public competition: [Comicstorian's complete story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Svag_l1Y8zw) was indexed with its exact title. Direct video fetching failed; no view count or full viewing is claimed. Established coverage means competition, not proof of likely reach. Searches also found a 2025 *One Last Time* video; that is a different miniseries and was excluded as a plot source.

The [licensed collection listing](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/693632/deadpool-kills-the-marvel-universe-by-cullen-bunn/), [Cullen Bunn interview](https://www.thenewestrant.com/2013/01/interview-time-cullen-bunn.html), [bounded plot recap](https://www.comicbasics.com/deadpool-kills-the-marvel-universe/), and indexed [issue-four listing](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/41554/deadpool_kills_the_marvel_universe_2011_4) establish this story. The Marvel URL uses a 2011 catalog label, while issue four and the collection were published in 2012. Do not confuse it with *Again* (2017), *One Last Time* (2025), or film continuity. Full issues were not read.

Three opening options were considered: “Why did Deadpool kill everyone?” is broad; “Deadpool found out he was fictional” gives away the mechanism; “Deadpool killed the Avengers. But they weren't his real target” starts with concrete conflict and reserves the last target for the ending. The selected original narration connects the failed brainwashing, mistaken liberation motive, attacks, Hulk's vulnerability and the search beyond the cast.

The geometric rig keeps the red suit, black eye patches, belt and twin sword handles consistent. Controlled regeneration, a shrinking device and crossing a page favor geometry. The actual collection cover supplies source recognition at near-native display scale. A persistent quiet Marvel theater provides channel framing. Paper marks the comic boundary, stone marks physical action, earth/olive identifies the swamp and daylight distinguishes the creators' workspace. No graphic injury is shown. The sparse **Echoes** bed leaves room for the late reveal; narration is locally synthesized, not an imitation of Ryan Reynolds. The subscribe request follows the reader reveal.
