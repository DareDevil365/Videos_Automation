# Why Doom Offered Doctor Strange to Mephisto

This Short retells the rescue in Roger Stern and Mike Mignola's *Doctor Strange and Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment* (1989). It follows the trade, Cynthia's refusal, Strange's escape and the personal cost of the rescue. The [brief](brief.json), [script](part-1.json), [series settings](series.json) and [source review](source-review.json) are the episode inputs.

The hook asks why Doom's own mother would reject her freedom. The answer depends on her refusal to benefit from another person's betrayal. Doom's hidden device helps Strange escape, while her refusal has redeemed her soul. The final line leaves Doom's intent open, as it is in the story. Public publisher summaries establish the team-up and rescue. The detailed rejection and device are corroborated in reading commentary. Full comic pages were not reviewed. [Marvel's story guide](https://www.marvel.com/amp/articles/comics/the-8-most-influential-doctor-strange-stories), [Marvel's Cynthia entry](https://www.marvel.com/comics/guides/2448/the_witches_of_the_marvel_universe), [William & Mary commentary](https://comicbookclub.pages.wm.edu/2024/02/21/doomed/)
