# Spider-Man Was Buried Alive. Someone Took His Place.

Host: Spider-Man was buried alive. For two weeks, someone else wore his suit.

Narrator: In Kraven's Last Hunt, the hunter wants more than a kill. He wants to prove he can be a better Spider-Man.

Narrator: He shoots Peter and buries him, then puts on a copy of his black costume. But Peter isn't dead. He's trapped in a deathlike state.

Narrator: Above ground, Kraven savagely attacks criminals. Newspapers say Spider-Man has gone berserk.

Narrator: Then he captures Vermin alone, a monster Peter once needed Captain America's help to defeat. To Kraven, that proves everything.

Narrator: But two weeks later, Peter wakes underground. He claws through the earth and climbs out of his own grave.

Narrator: He reaches Kraven, expecting another fight. Kraven refuses. He believes he's already won by becoming the stronger hunter.

Narrator: He even releases Vermin to test Peter, then stops the creature before it can kill him. Peter goes after Vermin.

Host: Kraven leaves a confession and dies by suicide. Peter survives the hunt, but being buried alive leaves him traumatized.
