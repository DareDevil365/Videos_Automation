## Selected narrative chain

Kraven wants to surpass Spider-Man → incapacitating and burying Peter removes his rival → impersonation lets Kraven demonstrate his own brutal version of the role → defeating Vermin becomes his claimed proof → Peter escapes and confronts him → Kraven refuses the expected rematch and stages one last test → the hunter’s fatal conclusion and Peter’s trauma make this a consequence story, not an uncomplicated victory montage.

The opening begins with confinement and identity theft. The source title appears during the next spoken beat, not a silent title card. The escape answers the physical question; the refusal/final test answers why the hunter took the suit. The ending does not celebrate suicide or depict a method. The shot plan shows confinement, a changing physical state and an aftermath instead of gore.
