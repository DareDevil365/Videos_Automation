# The Alien Who Took Thor's Hammer

An original narrated Short about Beta Ray Bill's introduction in Walt Simonson's *Thor* #337–339. The [brief](brief.json), [script and per-beat visual choices](part-1.json), [series settings](series.json) and [source review](source-review.json) are the inputs to the assistant-authored route.

## Research and reach

I considered a broad Thor origin, a list of characters who lifted Mjolnir, and one story about an unexpected wielder. I chose Bill's story because it opens with a familiar rule being tested and ends on a specific choice that explains the surprise. The audience can follow it without knowing the history of Asgard. [Marvel's reading guide](https://www.marvel.com/comics/guides/1479/8-best-stories-of-walt-simonsons-thor), [Simonson's account](https://www.twomorrows.com/media/MMV8SimonsonPreview.pdf)

A public search on November 6, 2026 showed about 104,000 views for a 2020 Beta Ray Bill news video and about 114,000 for a 2021 story recap from the same large comics channel. This is a rough signal that the character can interest viewers. These comparisons cover different stories and formats, and provide no estimate of this Short's reach. [News comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-WQ6RayeIYg), [story comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eoe0KvEwUv4)

## Shot decisions

| Beat | What the viewer sees |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | An alien profile, a recognizable hammer, and a burst of thunder. |
| Motive | Bill appears in front of the refugee fleet he protects. |
| Power | The hammer rises with Bill's grip and lightning gathers above it. |
| Rematch | The two characters close distance with bare hands. The palette switches to the fiery arena. |
| Reversal | Thor is in danger at the ledge. Bill reaches into frame, makes contact, and hauls him up. |
| Payoff | A second weapon rises over an anvil. Thor and Bill close with different hammers. |

The episode reuses the starter pack's hammer drawing, anchor-based hand staging, portrait compositing and camera choices. Thor's silver helmet and Bill's angular long face stay consistent. The ship and rescue blocking are original illustrations of the described events. The [shot decision guide](../../../docs/categories/comic-lore/SHOT_DECISIONS.md) explains how to choose these shots for a different episode.
