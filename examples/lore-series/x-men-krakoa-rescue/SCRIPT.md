An island captured the X-Men. Cyclops escaped, and brought it a second team.

**u2 — Narrator**

In Giant-Size X-Men number one, from 1975, Professor Xavier recruits new mutants to rescue his missing students.

**u3 — Narrator**

Storm, Wolverine and Nightcrawler join the rescue. They barely know each other, and Krakoa's jungle fights back.

**u4 — Narrator**

They find the missing team trapped inside. The monster isn't hiding on the island. It is the island.

**u5 — Narrator**

Krakoa feeds on mutant energy. It let Cyclops escape so he'd bring back more food. The rescue was another delivery.

**u6 — Narrator**

The captives are freed, but attacks can't defeat a whole landmass. Xavier tells them to combine their powers.

**u7 — Narrator**

Storm charges Polaris with lightning. Cyclops and Havok add their blasts, until Polaris can hurl Krakoa off the planet.

**u8 — Narrator**

The island leaves a deadly whirlpool. The mutants escape together, rescuers and rescued now one team.
