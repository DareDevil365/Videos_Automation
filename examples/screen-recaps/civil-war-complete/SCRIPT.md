Host: The Avengers win their battles. So why are they about to tear each other apart?

Narrator: Tony feels responsible for Sokovia and agrees. Steve fears political control. After Peggy's funeral, he follows his own conscience.

Narrator: Then a bombing kills Wakanda's king, and Bucky is blamed. His son, Black Panther, wants revenge. Steve finds Bucky first.

Narrator: A fake psychiatrist triggers Bucky's brainwashing. Steve stops his helicopter escape. Once himself again, Bucky reveals five more Winter Soldiers hidden in Siberia.

Narrator: Clint frees Wanda. Sharon returns Steve's gear. Steve gathers his allies, but Tony intercepts them at the airport. Spider-Man steals Steve's shield; Ant-Man goes giant.

Narrator: Natasha lets Steve and Bucky escape, then goes on the run. Vision accidentally shoots down Rhodey, leaving him paralyzed. Steve's other allies are sent to the Raft prison.

Narrator: Tony discovers Bucky was framed and follows them. In Siberia, they find the other soldiers already dead. Someone has led them into a different trap.

Narrator: The bomber, Zemo, lost his family in Sokovia. He plays a recording: a brainwashed Bucky murdered Tony's parents. Steve knew about the murder.

Narrator: Tony attacks Bucky. Steve won't abandon his friend. Tony blasts off Bucky's metal arm; Steve disables the armor, then leaves his shield behind.

Narrator: Black Panther stops Zemo's suicide and arrests him. Steve frees his friends. Tony helps Rhodey walk again. Bucky sleeps in Wakanda while they work to remove his brainwashing.
