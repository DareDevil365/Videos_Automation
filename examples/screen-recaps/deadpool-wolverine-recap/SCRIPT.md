# Deadpool & Wolverine: How Two Failures Saved a Timeline

**u1 — Host**

Deadpool's world is being erased. His plan is to steal another Wolverine.

**u2 — Narrator**

In Deadpool and Wolverine, Wade has quit hero work after the Avengers reject him and his relationship with Vanessa falls apart.

**u3 — Narrator**

TVA agent Paradox offers him a new life, then reveals his universe is dying because its anchor, Logan, is dead. Paradox plans to erase it faster.

**u4 — Narrator**

Wade digs up Logan, fights agents with the adamantium bones, then searches other realities. He returns with a Wolverine haunted by failing his X-Men.

**u5 — Narrator**

Paradox says a replacement won't work and dumps them in the Void. Cassandra Nova, Xavier's twin, kills their companion Johnny Storm. They escape Alioth.

**u6 — Narrator**

Wade admits he can't promise to repair Logan's past. Their partnership becomes a brutal car fight, until Laura and the resistance find them.

**u7 — Narrator**

Blade, Elektra, Gambit and Laura help storm Cassandra's base. Juggernaut's helmet blocks her powers, but Paradox's agent Pyro shoots her.

**u8 — Narrator**

Logan insists they save her. Cassandra opens a portal home, then learns about the Time Ripper and follows them, planning to destroy every timeline.

**u9 — Narrator**

Her Deadpool army kills Nicepool and blocks the street. Wade and Logan fight through, until Peter arrives and charms the variants.

**u10 — Narrator**

To stop the machine, someone must bridge matter and antimatter. Paradox says they'll die. Wade locks Logan out and takes both power lines.

**u11 — Narrator**

Logan breaks in and grabs Wade. Together they overload the machine, destroy Cassandra and survive by sharing the burden. Their timeline stabilizes.

**u12 — Host**

Paradox is arrested. Logan and Laura join Wade's family, and Wade reconnects with Vanessa. He couldn't undo Logan's past, but gave him a future. For more stories like this, subscribe.
