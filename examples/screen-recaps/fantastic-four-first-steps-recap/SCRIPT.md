# Fantastic Four: The Baby Galactus Wanted

**u1 — Host**

A planet-eater offers to spare Earth. His price is one family's baby.

**u2 — Narrator**

In The Fantastic Four: First Steps, four astronauts are already celebrated heroes. Reed and Sue are expecting their first child.

**u3 — Narrator**

The Silver Surfer warns that Galactus is coming. The team flies into space and watches him destroy another world before he brings them aboard.

**u4 — Narrator**

Galactus senses their unborn son's cosmic power. He offers Earth for the child, who could inherit his hunger. The family refuses and runs.

**u5 — Narrator**

The Surfer pursues their ship. Reed slingshots around a neutron star, buying their escape. During the chase, Sue gives birth to Franklin.

**u6 — Narrator**

Back on Earth, crowds demand the baby be surrendered. Sue refuses to sacrifice Franklin or abandon humanity. Reed plans to teleport the whole planet away.

**u7 — Narrator**

The world builds the bridges, but the Surfer destroys them. Johnny saves the last one and confronts Shalla-Bal: she serves Galactus to protect her own world.

**u8 — Narrator**

Recordings of the worlds she helped destroy break through her resolve. She flees. The family changes the plan: lure Galactus through the remaining bridge instead.

**u9 — Narrator**

New York evacuates underground through Mole Man's city. Franklin becomes the bait, but Galactus finds the real baby. Ben rescues the stretched Reed.

**u10 — Narrator**

Reed retrieves Franklin while Sue pushes Galactus toward the portal. Johnny prepares a final sacrifice, but Shalla-Bal takes his place and drives her master through.

**u11 — Narrator**

Sue collapses and dies from the effort. Then Franklin revives her. The family that refused to surrender one child has saved the world.

**u12 — Host**

Four years later, Sue finds a green-cloaked stranger beside Franklin: Doctor Doom. The film leaves his plan unanswered. For more stories like this, subscribe.
