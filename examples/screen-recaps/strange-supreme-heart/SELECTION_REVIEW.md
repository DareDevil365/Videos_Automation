The story is What If…? S1E4 (2021), not the original Doctor Strange film or a comic issue. The poster and opening identify it. The chain is loss → magic/time travel → failed attempts → absolute-point warning → demand for power → centuries of absorption → the other self’s resistance → a victory that destroys the reality needed to keep Christine alive.

The hook asks why Christine keeps dying; it does not reveal the destroyed universe. Red and purple cloaks distinguish the two selves. The split is introduced before the duel. A teacher and cracked clock explain the warning; the forbidden-book close-up motivates absorption. The final crystal contains two figures, then an isolated Strange at the phrase about being alone. The subscription card begins after that consequence.
