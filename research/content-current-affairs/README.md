- [WTO glossary](https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/glossary_e/tariffs_e.htm): tariff definition.

The distinction viewers must leave with: **Indian exports entering New Zealand and New Zealand exports entering India are different directions with different concessions.** Almost 57% describes export coverage, not a count of tariff lines. The revised closing test asks which direction goods are going and whether they qualify.
