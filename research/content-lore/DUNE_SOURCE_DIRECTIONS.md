The proposed “Odyssey character is the ancestor of Timothée's Dune character” video remains **unverified**. Separate an actor from the fictional character Paul Atreides, an etymological/mythological reference from literal genealogy, and the original novels from later expanded canon. Establish the exact passage and continuity before using that hook. Do not turn a name similarity into a factual shared-film-universe claim.

For the next source pass, obtain the relevant authorized novel excerpt or book chapter, record edition/chapter, check what is directly stated versus alluded to, and write a short explanation in original language. Avoid reproducing a long passage or treating a fan wiki as the sole evidence. Current film release dates/casting/box office require a separate fresh movie-source check.
