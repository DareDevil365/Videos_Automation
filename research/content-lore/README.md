## Research method for the software

Start with an official publisher event/character guide, then identify the exact run and numbered issues through its reading list. The accessible [official Secret Wars reading list](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/508/secret-wars) supplies ordered issue links. Use [Marvel Unlimited](https://www.marvel.com/unlimited) or lawfully obtained books for a later panel-level check when precision requires the full work. Public availability of a source is not a license to reproduce its scans, covers, logo or film clips.

Fan wikis, Reddit reading orders and unofficial metadata repositories are useful discovery leads. They should point back to a run, issue, chapter or publisher source before a disputed detail becomes narration. Avoid combining differently named versions of a character or silently treating a variant timeline as the main continuity. A fan theory needs an explicit theory label and support for what is known versus inferred.

The official publisher pages are sometimes blocked to automated direct fetches. This pass used the search reader's publisher text alongside an accessible publisher reading list. The brief records that limitation rather than pretending to contain a fresh full-page or comic-page snapshot. The parent production workflow must retain this access record and perform its normal source review; `editorial_verified` remains false.

## Visual and series decisions

Use original code-drawn diagrams: fractured world tiles, an abstract throne, a survival-craft schematic, a concealed power connection, a simple food icon, and worlds being reconstructed. Caption each with actual narration concepts. These are explanatory illustrations, not comic panels, authenticated screenshots or literal animated character performances. No image/video-generation service, unlicensed scan download or copied superhero logo is part of this packet.

Each part should answer a local question before pointing forward. The final part concludes this arc. A next-part link becomes a real link only after that export/publication exists; the manifest currently stores null URLs. The dedicated [comic_lore preset](../../docs/categories/comic-lore/HOW_TO_CREATE.md) supplies local portrait illustrations and measured speech. The raw brief remains unverified by itself; an exact script/brief-bound provided review supports these prototypes only.

Before calling a result good, have a cold audience critic identify its first confusing term, first likely swipe-away moment, whether each part pays off its title, whether the visuals clarify the cause, and whether the serial handoff feels earned. The critic should cite actual timestamps/lines and distinguish its opinion from measured viewer retention.
