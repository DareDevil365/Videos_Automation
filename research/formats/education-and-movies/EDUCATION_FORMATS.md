# Educational and productivity Shorts: research catalog

Checked: 2026-11-01. Research output only; no videos, accounts, API calls to generation providers, or publication jobs were created. Formats and hooks below are proposed experiments, not demonstrated trends or promises of views, employment, confidence, or income.

## Recommended channel promise

“One useful move you can actually see working.” Serve students and early-career people with short, concrete demonstrations across learning, getting work done, communication, jobs, and AI. A named series identifies the subject before the viewer commits: **Study Lab**, **Start Small**, **Career Proof**, **AI Without the Hype**, or **Money Claims Checked**. This is an editorial recommendation, not evidence that all these audiences will overlap. Measure series separately and narrow the channel if their audiences diverge.

The production system supplies narration, fictional scenarios, diagrams, mock chats, synthetic sample data, and genuine tool demonstrations. The user does not need to film, write, record a voice, or invent success stories. A live software demonstration is eligible only after the system has actually executed and checked the steps; a diagram must not masquerade as a recorded product UI.

## Evidence and free-tool findings

- **Study techniques:** Dunlosky and colleagues' review assessed practice testing and distributed practice as broadly useful. It does not establish a universal review timetable or guarantee grades. Use a small demonstration of retrieval or spacing, with a source note, rather than “this makes you a genius.” [Peer-reviewed review, APS](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/pspi/1529100612453266/).
- **Starting tasks:** research supports examining specific if–then plans for goal pursuit. The short should distinguish goal-attainment evidence from proof of a cure for procrastination. An original wording template is an editorial adaptation. [Sheeran, Listrom and Gollwitzer, implementation-intention meta-analysis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/10463283.2024.2334563); [2006 author publication record](https://www.socmot.uni-konstanz.de/publications/implementation-intentions-and-goal-achievement-meta-analysis-effects-and-processes). The 2024 publisher page was search-indexed but direct retrieval was restricted; obtain the full paper before numerical effect claims.
- **Confidence:** self-efficacy is a specific research construct, not a synonym for universal charisma. Task practice can inspire an episode, but do not claim a particular communication drill is clinically validated, treats anxiety, or works for everyone. [Bandura's original paper record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/847061/).
- **Job applications:** STAR is an official career-guidance structure for describing situation, task, action, and result. Use fictional demonstration answers and instruct viewers to supply their real experience. It is a UK guidance source, not a guarantee of global hiring outcomes. [National Careers Service](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice/the-star-method).
- **Earning/job claims:** the FTC describes fake-check, reshipping, advance-payment, and other employment scams. Scripts can illustrate documented patterns without accusing an unverified named company. No earnings should be invented. [FTC job-scam guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams).
- **Free AI:** Gemini's current pricing lists some free API model/tool combinations, not unlimited access to every feature. Check the exact model, tool, country, quota, and data terms before an episode or production run. [Official pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing), [limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits).
- **Current product naming matters:** the official NotebookLM plan URL now redirects to **Gemini Notebook**. Standard signup is free, subject to eligibility and usage limits. Do not publish an old tool name/UI tutorial without checking the current interface. No supported free unattended automation integration was verified in this research. [Official plans](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16213268).
- **Free learning is not free certification:** Microsoft advertises free learning paths/training; this does not establish that every exam, lab resource, or credential is free. [Microsoft Learn](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-learn).
- **AI instruction:** Google documents constraints, examples, and response-format guidance. Demonstrate an actual input/output comparison; do not turn one output into a general model-quality claim. [Official prompt-design guidance](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/prompting-strategies).

These sources support the subjects or mechanisms. They do not demonstrate current Shorts demand. No competitor view counts, retention, RPM, or earnings were observed or inferred in this catalog.

## Format catalog

**Pilot candidate** means a suitable first experiment after claim/asset checks, not publication approval. **Needs verification** identifies a dependency that has not been demonstrated. “High automation fit” is an engineering estimate; no renderer or voice pipeline has been benchmarked.

## Three recommended pilot formats

1. **F02 Prompt makeover.** Clear before/after visuals and a useful takeaway, with original commentary. Pilot: EDU-017. Only proceed after genuinely free access and a recorded response comparison are verified.
2. **F06 Retrieval with feedback.** Straightforward original graphics and a complete learning interaction; research supports the broader method. Pilot: EDU-001. It is a content-format experiment, not a test of a viewer's long-term learning.
3. **F05 Interview answer repair.** A concrete fictional role-play, supported by official guidance, with no need for live job listings or borrowed speaker footage. Pilot: EDU-010. Results/credentials remain fictional and clearly identified.

These prioritize verifiable substance, local production, and little dependence on third-party footage. They are not rankings of market size, virality, or earnings. Reusing the same visual framework is a production convenience; each episode still needs materially distinct teaching value. [YouTube monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en).

## Material risks and user decisions

All requested pillars remain in the catalog. These are disclosures for choosing the editorial approach; there is no claim that a user accepting a risk supplies missing factual evidence, copyright permission, or platform eligibility.

| Area | Material risk | Decision to make before the pilot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money and jobs | Viewers may act on an outdated offer, hidden fee, misleading income expectation, or country-specific requirement. | Prefer durable skill demonstrations or take on the extra source-refresh work of current opportunity coverage. No verified opening or income evidence exists in this backlog yet. |
| Confidence and procrastination | A brief exercise can be mistaken for universally effective mental-health treatment or can shame people for difficulties it does not address. | Choose practical, specific rehearsal/planning language and disclose limitations; stronger clinical claims require a different evidence/review process. |
| Study advice | Technique-level research does not validate a universal schedule, subject, or exam outcome. | Use demonstrations and illustrative plans, or conduct a properly scoped additional evidence review before precise effectiveness claims. |
| AI tools and free access | Models, names, quotas, feature availability, and data terms can change; recorded demonstrations may become obsolete. | Choose evergreen workflow lessons or budget a recurring recheck for dated product tutorials. Only verified free dependencies enter production. |
| Channel coherence | Students, job seekers, and AI-tool viewers may not form one stable audience. | Pilot three named series and narrow based on actual results rather than assume their overlap. |
| Unattended publication | Automation can produce incorrect explanations, unusable demos, or false source links. | Decide the initial review arrangement. If no review is wanted, uncertain jobs remain held rather than presented as checked. |
| Monetization | Substantially original instruction may still be assessed unfavorably; repetitive presentation can undermine value. | Choose whether to test the proposed original formats, acknowledging that no agent can certify YPP approval. |
