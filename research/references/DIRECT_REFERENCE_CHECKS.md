## Implications for our plan

1. A sigma edit and a software-cost-saving promotion genuinely exist in the Shorts interface with substantial visible accumulated reach. That establishes individual public outcomes, not a reliably repeatable production recipe.
2. Paid-promotion disclosure is visible on the two large examples. It does not establish the payment amount, organic reach, YouTube ad eligibility, or our access to a similar sponsor. Genspark is an observed commercial brand/topic example, not a secured deal or a verified affiliate program for this project.
3. The AI Short uses a related-video path to longer content. This is concrete evidence of a Shorts-to-longer-video route, not proof of its sales conversion. It informs an optional later education funnel while keeping an initially Shorts-only plan viable.
4. The low-view movie reference does not verify the proposed nine-film cards, poster/GIF-only layout or that such formats fail. Exact visual references for those variants remain a research gap; a local production pilot can test feasibility without inventing demand.
5. None of the samples establishes US audience share. The US-first strategy remains a content/audience hypothesis and must be tested with owned-channel reports.

This was a short visual and metadata check, not a complete content, audio, legal or revenue audit. No claim is made that all reference videos or every catalog format was watched. The source URLs support these observations; no material should be copied merely because it was viewable.
