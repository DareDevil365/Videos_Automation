# Shorts source research and risk register

Checked: 2026-11-01. Research only; no footage acquired, rights cleared for a particular edit, accounts enrolled, or videos posted. This report supports the user's choice of risk appetite. It does not silently exclude proposed formats because they are risky.

## How to read this register

`Rule` identifies a sourced platform or provider condition. `Assessment` is our conditional judgment; no numerical probabilities are available. `Mitigation` is a project recommendation, not a claim that every platform requires human review. The table below preserves the initial research snapshot; the **current user choices are in [DECISIONS.md](../strategy/DECISIONS.md)** and [source/strike supervision update](STRIKE_PREVENTION_AND_SUPERVISION.md). Some mitigation directions are now selected; unresolved matters must not be treated as accepted. User acceptance cannot grant another person's rights, override service rules, or guarantee monetization. A format can be technically producible, permitted by a source, and still unsuitable for YouTube monetization.

## Proposed research output contract

For every future asset/format, report separately: `technical_feasibility`, `permission_evidence`, `source_scope`, `music_and_game_rights`, `platform_rule`, `risk_assessment`, `mitigations`, `user_choice`, `unresolved_questions`, `checked_at`, and `next_verification`. Never replace `user_choice: undecided` with a model recommendation. Keep risky ideas in the catalog; recommendations and third-party requirements are separate fields.

No current autonomous campaign integration was verified. Research can proceed through permitted public pages; production and platform participation require a separately supported method. The user's acceptance of editorial or business risk does not itself authorize accounts, publication, signing campaign terms, payments or contacting rights holders.
