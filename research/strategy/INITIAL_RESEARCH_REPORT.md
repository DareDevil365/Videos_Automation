## 1. Recommendation

Operate two separate channel experiments, not one feed mixing unrelated audiences:

- **Entertainment:** creator moments, storytime, fictional chat/comeback humor, sigma edits, game/animation combinations, movie recommendations, mysteries and pop-culture controversies. Research all requested variants; choose a small coherent series for the initial test.
- **Education/productivity:** one useful takeaway a viewer can see: AI tasks/free tools, study exercises, task initiation, communication/confidence, job preparation, and realistic earning-workflow explanations. Use named series so different interests remain identifiable.

Start with a small production and audience pilot rather than commit to all formats at once. The recommended first tests are chat comedy, original fictional storytime and one source-dependent entertainment format; education starts with an AI task/prompt comparison, a study retrieval exercise and an interview-answer repair. The source-dependent entertainment slot can be the user's movie-list format or a creator/podcast format, depending on the selected risks and actual source feasibility. This is a recommendation, not a choice made for the user.

Research has found individual high-reach examples, real clipping campaigns and plausible no-filming production paths. It has **not** established a universal formula, a currently underserved US market, typical success rates, an available sponsor contract or a positive earnings date.

## 4. The nine-movie example, made concrete

Working promise: **“9 movies worth watching—help pick number 10.”** Choose a coherent theme, not nine unrelated posters. The system researches film facts and curation, writes the cards, generates narration if used, creates the edit and proposes packaging.

One proposed timeline is a two-second opening, nine roughly four-second cards, then a brief closing question: about 40–50 seconds. Each card supplies title/year and a specific spoiler-free reason or concise verdict. The minimal “peak/goated” version stays in the catalog as a higher-risk thin-content variant. The comment prompt is optional audience participation; the nine promised picks are delivered without withholding an answer. Do not fabricate comments or claim a real vote before collecting one.

Poster/GIF/audio rights still matter. Free noncommercial movie-data access is not automatically a usable commercial poster feed. Film availability must be dated and country-specific if we mention where to watch. No actual films, images, affiliate offer or streaming listings were selected by this research.

## 5. Audience and cross-market opportunity

Keep the user's US-first preference. For education, design US-relevant examples and products; for entertainment, test relevant English/pop-culture framing while comparing available UK/Canada/Australia results. Do not assume those audiences are interchangeable or exclude other viewers solely because of presumed RPM.

The cross-market strategy is to identify a repeatable attention structure in one language/market, check destination competition, and create a culturally adapted original episode. Format inspiration is not permission for another creator's script/footage/music. Do not infer viewer location from language or call an already common English format untapped. ## 6. Revenue order, timing and amounts

Keep three clipping businesses separate: YouTube ad revenue on a clip channel; approved campaign compensation; and client-paid editing services. They have different access, content and labor requirements.

For entertainment, investigate eligible clipping campaigns alongside original formats intended for later YPP. For education, investigate one relevant affiliate and one stable validated template, then suitable sponsorships after audience evidence. Services, courses, memberships and live features can add continuing obligations that conflict with minimal owner involvement. Other-platform rewards remain optional and eligibility-specific.

Concrete published-rate arithmetic exists: a researched podcast campaign quoted $0.75/1K YouTube views; another promotional split-screen campaign quoted $1.50/1K for YouTube. If the applicable 10% creator fee applies, 100K actually counted approved views yield $67.50 or $135 before remaining fees/tax. These are specific briefs, not general rates for random clips. No enrollment, source access or account approval has occurred. [Campaign details and limits](../revenue/REVENUE_CAMPAIGNS_AND_YOUTUBE.md).

Aspire's survey median quoted Shorts base rates were $200 at its 2.5K–25K tier and $250 at 25K–60K. These are existing creators' quotes, not assured offers. A directly inspected Genspark-promotional Short establishes brand/topic fit, not the fee it earned. Potential education sponsor categories are writing, AI, study and productivity tools; entertainment prospects include games, media launches, networks and suitable apps. No company is committed to this project. [Benchmark and conditional revenue model](US_FIRST_REVENUE_PLAN.md).

| Planning window from implementation | Work and conditional money example | Budget treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | Prototype/source/account feasibility; no income modeled | $0 |
| Weeks 3–6 | Pilot; if a permitted $0.75/1K campaign counts 50K views, $33.75 after 10% | $0 until approved/settled; no guaranteed growth |

These are workload windows and arithmetic scenarios, not a prediction that the channel will attain those views or deals on that schedule. They are monthly flow examples, not cumulative totals. Bank timing adds approval, holding, verification and transfer delays. Do not count views as money, combine fees inconsistently or assume every campaign view also earns YPP money.

Ads are not the first-income budget assumption. Current and announced February 2027 YPP conditions materially affect a six-month plan and are separated in the revenue report. [Official announced changes](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009). No channel-specific RPM or reliable positive expected income can be estimated before our own data.

## 8. What the system will do

The saved research roles are: entertainment researcher, education researcher, source/risk reviewer and coordinating research lead. They can be reused for later research cycles; they do not keep running after this chat and no recurring schedule was created. [Role briefs and usage](../../agents/README.md).

The proposed local application connects these responsibilities:

**Topic/source discovery → claim/asset records → script and scene plan → narration/visuals → vertical render/captions/cover → quality and source checks → authorized publication → production monitoring → official performance/revenue review → next experiment.**

Use Python for orchestration, a local dashboard for previews/decisions, SQLite for durable job status and local files for media. FFmpeg composes/encodes videos; generated text/graphics/animations avoid requiring user filming. Free AI adapters have a verified local fallback or queue work when quota ends. Keeping the first runtime local avoids paid hosting and makes errors traceable. Exact models, voice licenses and throughput still need testing. Architecture and alternatives: [project guide](../../PROJECT_GUIDE.md).

Scripts must retain factual claim sources and asset provenance. For gossip/mysteries, mark confirmed events, allegations, rumors, interpretations and fiction distinctly. A source article is not copied wholesale. A diagram does not masquerade as a real software demo or leaked chat. These checks are work assigned to the system, not a request for the user to produce footage.

## 9. Execution plan and evidence needed

### Before public pilot: prove production

1. Select three test formats per channel and record the still-open source/risk choices. Keep the full catalog available for future cycles.
2. Establish one actual source route per selected format: permitted creator assets or eligible original graphics/animation. For movies, investigate a commercial image/data route instead of assuming a free developer key clears posters.
3. Test one free writer, one commercially permitted voice and one renderer. Save a complete private sample for each format and measure actual time, memory, intelligibility, readability and failures.
4. Connect channels separately only within the authorized implementation task. Verify correct channel IDs, private upload, processing status and recovery from an uncertain upload result before public scheduling.

### First ready-to-publish cycle: three formats × three episodes per channel

Produce **18 distinct Shorts total**, nine per channel, for example three per channel per week over three weeks. This is a manageable experiment proposal, not an algorithm requirement. Rotate formats across planned slots and allow every final episode its complete observation window. If build/source gates take longer, move the calendar rather than pretend the pilot started.

Review each upload at 24 hours, 72 hours and seven days. Use available official engaged views, continuation/watch measures, subscribers, traffic, geography and monetization state; keep missing/delayed reports unknown. Raw starts/replays are not completed viewing or cash. Some retention/geography reports will not exist for small/short samples.

### Days 31–60 of an active pilot

Check repeatability with new episodes, not cloned uploads. Retain at most two promising series per channel or call the result inconclusive. Investigate one real income route per channel, resolve actual payout eligibility and recheck campaign status. Prepare a sponsor media kit only from actual audience evidence; do not promise a deal by Day 60.

### Days 61–90

Decide which series can sustain distinct value, reliable free production, manageable risk and a real commercial route. Expand only within verified source supply and quota. If owner effort is too high, simplify the operation or cadence. A longer video or extra language is a later experiment, not an automatic scaling step.

The detailed measurement plan separates exact elapsed snapshots from calendar-day reports, source changes from editorial changes, and forecast/booked/pending/approved/settled/bank income. It specifies no invented viral score, minimum completion percentage, guaranteed country share or universal best posting hour. [Full pilot protocol](PILOT_MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md).
