# Short-video writing

Serve the chosen genre and the owner's premise. Make the audience want a specific answer, then deliver an answer the story earned. Curiosity, surprise, recognition and anticipation are options, not a compulsory alarming first line. Do not promise engagement or retention results from craft rules.

Choose the relevant reference: [comedy](references/comedy.md), [mystery/thriller](references/mystery.md), [emotional/cinematic story](references/story.md), or [aura captions](references/aura.md). For factual explainers use [evidence-led writing](references/factual.md). Read [timing](references/timing.md) when planning spoken or chat delivery.

Before dialogue, identify the protagonist's immediate want, opposing pressure, opening question, early plant and ending. Explain the causal chain to yourself without the title, soundtrack or an invented fact. If the explanation needs a new rule at the ending, rebuild the premise. When the application asks for alternatives, vary the mechanism rather than renaming characters in the same idea.

In chat, every story fact must come from a message, visible phone occurrence or established contact/thread context. Give each person a different want and reaction pattern; changing names is insufficient. Track what each private conversation lets them know. A narrator should not read speaker names, timestamps, directions or a separate explanation. Preserve natural fragments and contractions. The last meaningful reply must change the situation or fulfill the opening rather than explain the joke, recap the reveal or add a generic moral.

Revise at the level of cause and consequence. Remove a line if deleting it changes nothing. Replace repeated disbelief with a choice, new evidence, a consequence or a reversal. Read the exact words aloud; split a sentence where thought or intention changes, never just to meet a message count. Keep the selected format's schema, word and unit bounds. Treat stock assets as the available inventory, not permission to invent unseen events.

Critique the conversation without its writer's sales pitch. Reconstruct the question/answer, identify necessary assumptions, and cite exact lines for opening, cause, knowledge, progression, character voice, payoff and genre. State what a weak line fails to accomplish. A fluent model verdict is not proof of humor, suspense, audience response or human approval. Use the application's bounded rewrite/recheck route rather than an endless polishing loop.

Timing belongs in supported delivery fields or editor configuration, never hidden control tags in spoken dialogue. Protect the complete line and the viewer's reading time. Use an earned pause before an interpretation changes; do not place the same long pause before every reply. Music and sound should support attention without supplying a missing cause or telegraphing every punchline.
