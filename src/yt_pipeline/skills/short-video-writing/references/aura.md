# Aura and performance captions

Build around an actual permitted action or transformation. Choose the visible decisive moment first. Short captions should sharpen a contrast: noise versus concentration, hesitation versus a completed attempt, or practice versus payoff. Remove captions that merely announce an invented rating or repeat the same confidence slogan.

Respect source identity. Different stock athletes are a montage, not one person's biography. Do not attribute invented speech or achievements to a recognizable person. Describe what is visible rather than fabricate a before/after story the footage does not show.

Give the action room. Cut toward the real impact, land a brief final caption at the supported peak, then leave before empty footage or an accidental short-clip loop. A freeze is allowed when it is sourced from the actual action and identified in production evidence. Music can supply pulse; do not claim beat synchronization unless actual chosen timestamps were measured. These captions need not have a narrator, joke or mystery ending.
