# Comic lore in short chapters

Tell the events as a chain of actions, choices and consequences. Start with a concrete question whose answer lands in this chapter. Introduce a name with its role, and keep aliases only where the distinction matters. Use the supplied source claims for the chosen comic run. Let suspense come from the character's problem and the unfolding facts.

Plan the chapter's turn before its hook. When the request specifies a continuing serialized story, earlier parts end on a concrete cliffhanger and only the final part concludes the overall arc. A chapter still needs real progression before it reaches the unresolved event. For an individual-answer series, deliver that chapter's answer before the next-part lead. Give the viewer one new event per beat. Replace a second statement of the same theme with the action that proves it. A short clause and a longer sentence can form a rhythm. Frequent full stops can make synthetic narration sound halting, so use natural sentence structures and keep complete names together.

When a host is requested, use speaker Host for the first and last units, with Narrator between them. The host opens on the hook and then names the series and part. The storyteller carries the action. Deliver a specific chapter payoff before the host returns. The outro can name the next part and invite a subscription in one short line.

Use the request's series part count to distinguish the middle chapter from the finale. Check where a continuation link will actually work. Local preview descriptions can link to the next MP4. For YouTube Shorts, description URLs are not clickable. A release outro can direct the viewer to the related video after that relation has been configured. Never invent an uploaded URL, a next part that does not exist or a completed channel action.

Cold-read the exact spoken text. Identify the first sentence whose removal would improve momentum. Test whether a newcomer can identify who did what and why the ending follows. Check whether the chapter reaches the promised turn by the penultimate beat. A cliffhanger should make the unresolved next step clear. Read the final reply without the title or soundtrack to detect a repeated takeaway.

The local editor measures speech before setting shot lengths. Tight narration trims sustained quiet sections inside synthesized clips and leaves short natural breaks. Pauses, speed, music levels and cues belong in supported editor settings. Keep the written dialogue free of timing tags.
